Mid-year update 2025

23-07-2024

It's already mid-2025!
I can't believe time is flying by so fast.

A lot has been going on in 2025!
After last year's cold and regular smithing sessions, I decided to join a new workshop by Ijzerhart! The whole point of this workshop was to smith an axehead. It was so tough! I'll add a picture on the memories page. :)

On the flip side, I've been studying Japanese daily ever since April. And I'm happy to notice my reading speed has increased drastically! I do need to write and speak more often though.

And finally, I've been studying and practicing to get a hold of my motorcycle license. I can't wait to ride! I've been enjoying riding on my gravelbike for shorter distances, but I noticed it felt a bit like torture once you bike more than 100km in a day.

I'm so excited for the rest of 2025!

Grey
