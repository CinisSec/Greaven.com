Akumuchan the movie, Gachiakuta(1) and Galaxias(1)

14-08-2025

It's not been a month since my last blogpost but I wanted to write this down to give myself a pat on the back.

Ever since I started learning Japanese I've been trying to read some books and manga in Japanese. Which was extremely painful due to my slow reading speed and lack of comprehension. But recently I have been noticing my increased reading speed and boost in reading comprehension! Learning a language from scratch like Japanese really humbles me about the complexity of languages and their place in our world. Ever since tackling Japanese I have met amazing people with whom I can share my love for traditional craftmanship and japanese,chinese culture.

But let's get back to the books!

The book I'm currently reading is "Akumuchan The Movie". As you can infer from the name of the book it is an novelisation of the titular movie. I thought it would be a good exercise to watch the movie first to have a general grasp of the story. It was quite funny to see Gackt take a role as the dream prince protecting one of our protagonists. You see as a teenager I used to be a big fan of Gackt. But nowadays that mostly faded and transformed in some kind of nostalgia for his persona. While watching the movie I couldn't shake the feeling that some things were definitely off. The main cast is really great, they have great chemistry. But the new boy added to the story felt somewhat out of place. His acting was all over the place which truly brought down the whole movie as the plot revolves around him. Meanwhile when I am reading the book I don't really feel the same about this character. It could be because of my lack of reading comprehension when it comes to nuance. So for now I'm really enjoying reading "Akumuchan The movie". And since it's part of the kodansha tsubasa bunko line of books it is quite easy to pick up and read.

I was also reading kimagure robotto but I've put it on pause before reading anything in this blogpost! When I started reading it, it would take me 4 hours to read a page. I was really out of my depth on this one.

On the manga side, I've finished reading the first volume of Gachiakuta and Galaxias. Both are pretty close on the shonen spectrum but they have very different ways of portraying their world. I'm really curious about both. I just laughed a bit more when reading Galaxias.

Gachiakuta is really great at setting up the world. The mix of graffiti art and manga is really refreshing to contemplate. I have a real soft spot when it comes to gritty and expressive lines when reading manga.

Galaxias on the other hand is bit more understated and cozy. The art is really beautiful but I don't think it's all that justified to compare the two. They're just very different worlds.

I wonder what both of those worlds will bring to the table! In the meantime I'll be reading some other books!

Thank you for reading!

Grey
